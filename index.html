How I got to v25

After playing around with different form factors I realized that I loved the interactive nature of v11, which was a museum. However, for the purposes of this product I felt a library was more fitting. I then made a page for Harper Library and tried to model off of that a bit.

After building, I spent a few versions refining details and pulling together things I liked from previous designs. Notably, I kept the old-school library feel while porting over the Apple liquid glass design from v04.

I'm really happy with how it turned out — but still have work to do!
